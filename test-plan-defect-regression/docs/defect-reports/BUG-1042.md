# BUG-1042 — Title of exactly 80 characters is rejected

- **ID:** BUG-1042
- **Status:** Open
- **Reported:** 2026-10-12
- **Observed in:** New task form
- **Environment:** Tasklane demo, Chrome on macOS, 1440×900 viewport
- **Frequency:** Every time

## Expected behavior

The product specification allows a task title between 1 and 80 characters after trimming. A title containing exactly 80 characters should be accepted and the task should be created.

## Actual behavior

Submitting a title of exactly 80 characters keeps the form open and displays the error “Title must be 80 characters or fewer.” Removing one character allows the same task to be saved.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open Tasklane and choose **Reset demo data**.
2. Choose **New task**.
3. Enter this 80-character title:

   ```text
   Review onboarding checklist copy and layout on desktop, tablet, and mobile views
   ```

4. Leave all other fields at their defaults.
5. Choose **Create task**.

## Impact

Users cannot save titles at the documented upper boundary even though the interface says the limit is 80 characters. They must shorten valid content or choose less descriptive wording. This affects both new tasks and editing an existing task to an 80-character title.

## Additional context

- A 79-character title saves successfully.
- No browser console error appears.
- Reproduced twice with a fresh reset and once with copied text from a plain-text editor.
